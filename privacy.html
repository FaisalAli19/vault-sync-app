Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Vault Sync is a private tool used only by its owner.

Data it accesses

With the owner's Google sign-in, it has read-only access to the owner's Google Calendar events and YouTube account data such as playlists and videos.

How the data is used and stored

The data is saved as notes on the owner's own devices. Sign-in tokens are stored on the owner's computer. There is no server, database, analytics or advertising.

Sharing

The data is not sold or shared with anyone. Its use of data from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from Google Account permissions.
